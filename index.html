Read me
  • • 1 minute in-game means 1 second in real life.
  • • There are 7 statuses that you need to see, namely: hunger, hydration, happiness, hygiene, and Health Point.
  • • If one of the status bars runs out (for example, hunger, hydration, or happiness), the health point will be reduced. Make sure to fill out all of the statuses!
  • • There are random events in the game, if you accomplish the event, you will get a reward.
  • • The game will be over if the Health Point (HP) is touching 0.
  • • Have fun!
